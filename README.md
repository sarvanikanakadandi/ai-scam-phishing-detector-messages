# 🛡️ AI Scam & Phishing Detector

An AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to detect and analyze **scams, phishing attempts, suspicious messages, emails, SMS/WhatsApp messages, and potentially malicious URLs**.

The system uses **Google Gemini AI** to analyze digital communication and provide an understandable risk assessment to help users identify potential cyber threats before taking action.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

With the increasing use of online banking, digital payments, social media, email, and messaging applications, cyber scams and phishing attacks have become common.

Attackers often use:

- Urgent messages
- Fake rewards
- Account suspension threats
- OTP requests
- Password requests
- Banking information requests
- Fake KYC updates
- Suspicious links
- Impersonation
- Emotional manipulation

The **AI Scam & Phishing Detector** analyzes these patterns and provides users with a risk assessment.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Detect potential scam and phishing messages.
- Analyze suspicious emails.
- Identify suspicious URLs.
- Analyze SMS and WhatsApp messages.
- Provide an AI-generated risk score.
- Explain why content may be dangerous.
- Provide practical safety advice.
- Educate users about common cyber scams.
- Create an easy-to-use cybersecurity awareness platform.

---

# ✨ Features

## 🔍 AI Message Scanner

Users can enter suspicious messages and analyze them using Google Gemini AI.

The system checks for:

- 🚨 Urgency and pressure
- 😨 Threats and fear
- 💰 Requests for money
- 🔐 Password or OTP requests
- 🏦 Banking information
- 🔗 Suspicious links
- 🎁 Fake rewards
- 👤 Impersonation
- 📥 Suspicious downloads
- 🧑 Personal information requests
- 🧠 Social engineering techniques
- 😟 Emotional manipulation
